# Brand

UnderCover's look is a 1960s airline ticket office: confident blue and white, bold type, and crisp lines. One strong blue does most of the work and the other colors support it.

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Headings and body**: Libre Franklin. Headings are bold (800 or 900) with slightly tight letter spacing
- **Labels, table headings, buttons and taglines**: Libre Franklin in bold capitals with wide letter spacing
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: JetBrains Mono

## Colors

- **Primary**: Sky Blue `#0A5CB8`, white text
- **Secondary**: Gold `#F2B705`, Navy text
- **Tertiary**: Horizon `#6FA8DC`, Navy text
- **Information**: Steel `#5B7083`, white text
- **Highlight or alert**: Signal Red `#C8102E`, white text
- **Ink on light backgrounds**: Navy `#0B1F3A`
- **Ink on dark backgrounds**: Pale Blue `#E6EEF8`

Use Sky Blue, Gold and Horizon when three things sit side by side, such as a claim's states or chart series. Add Steel fourth and Signal Red fifth.

## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `#F4F7FB`, white panels and cards
- **Dark mode**: page background `#071426`, Navy `#0B1F3A` panels and cards

The app's header is a solid Sky Blue band with white text. Panels have small corners (4 to 6px) and a hairline outline in `#D6DEE8` in light mode or `#1E3457` in dark mode. Buttons are solid Sky Blue with white capitals and small corners. Status labels are small rectangles with bold capitals.

Sky Blue is too dark for text on dark backgrounds, so use Horizon there. Gold is too bright for text on light backgrounds, so use it only as a fill behind Navy text.

## Avoid

- Logos, globes or emblems that look like a real airline's
- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Gradient fills, script fonts and retro textures such as film grain
- Large rounded corners and pill-shaped buttons

## Tone

Plain business English in US spelling. Short labels, no jargon and no exclamation marks. Money is in US dollars, such as $1,250.00.
